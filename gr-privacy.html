User Data policy

Health Data (Heart Rate)

Ghostracer requests the READ_HEART_RATE permission on Wear OS devices. With your permission, the Ghostracer Wear OS app reads your heart rate from your watch's sensors while you are recording a running or cycling activity. Heart rate data is only accessed while an activity is being recorded and is used solely to display your current heart rate during the activity and to save it with that activity's record so you can review it afterwards.

Heart rate data is sent from your watch to the Ghostracer app on your paired phone and is stored only on your device. It is not uploaded to, collected by, or accessible to me, and it is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, analytics or any purpose other than recording your activity.

You may choose to export or upload an activity, including its heart rate data, to a third-party service such as Strava. This only happens when you explicitly initiate the upload, and once uploaded the data is handled under that service's own privacy policy.

Heart rate access is optional. You can deny or revoke the permission at any time in your watch's settings and still record activities without heart rate. You can delete saved activities and their heart rate data from within the app, and uninstalling the app removes all stored data from your device.

Location Data

Ghostracer requests access to your device's precise location (GPS) on your phone and Wear OS watch. With your permission, the app reads your location while you are recording a running or cycling activity, including when the screen is off or the app is in the background, so that the full route can be recorded. A notification is shown while an activity is being recorded. Location is not accessed when no activity is being recorded.

Location data is used solely to measure your distance, speed and pace, to show your route on a map, to race against your previous activities, and to save the route with that activity's record. It is stored only on your device. It is not uploaded to, collected by, or accessible to me, and it is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, analytics or any purpose other than recording your activity.

You may choose to export or upload an activity, including its route, to a third-party service such as Strava. This only happens when you explicitly initiate the upload, and once uploaded the data is handled under that service's own privacy policy. Note that a route may reveal places such as your home.

You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings, although activities cannot be recorded without it. You can delete saved activities and their routes from within the app, and uninstalling the app removes all stored data from your device.
